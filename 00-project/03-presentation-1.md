## 1. Project Introduction

Podcast production often involves multiple participants recorded using separate microphones and cameras.

These recordings must be synchronised, organised, edited, and combined into a final video.

Managing multiple recordings manually can be complicated and time-consuming.

The Multitrack Podcast Video Editor aims to simplify this process through a dedicated desktop application.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Traditional video-editing workflows can be unnecessarily complicated for podcast production.

The main challenges include:

- Managing multiple audio and video recordings.
- Synchronising recordings from different devices.
- Organising recordings by participant.
- Removing unwanted conversation segments.
- Selecting the appropriate camera view.
- Mixing multiple audio sources.
- Exporting the final podcast efficiently.

A dedicated application could simplify these tasks.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a desktop application specifically designed for editing multitrack podcast recordings.

The application will allow users to:

- Create and manage podcast editing projects.
- Import audio and video recordings.
- Assign recordings to individual participants.
- Synchronise recordings on a shared timeline.
- Remove unwanted sections.
- Select visible video tracks.
- Mix audio tracks.
- Preview the edited podcast.
- Export the final composition locally.

The goal is to provide a practical and accessible editing workflow.

---

## 4. Project Objectives

### Primary Objective

Develop a functional desktop application that simplifies multitrack podcast video editing.

### Specific Objectives

1. Design an intuitive desktop user interface.
2. Implement project creation and management.
3. Support importing multiple audio and video files.
4. Organise recordings by participant.
5. Provide manual recording synchronisation.
6. Implement timeline-based editing.
7. Support audio mixing and video-track selection.
8. Provide a preview of the edited composition.
9. Enable local video and audio export.
10. Support saving and reopening editing projects.

---

## 5. Target Users

The intended users include:

- Podcast creators.
- Content creators.
- Interview producers.
- Small media production teams.
- Students working with recorded discussions.

The application will focus on users who need to combine multiple recordings without relying on unnecessarily complex editing workflows.

---

## 6. Main Functional Requirements

### Project Management

Users should be able to create, open, save, and manage editing projects.

### Media Management

Users should be able to import recordings and associate them with participants.

### Timeline Editing

Users should be able to synchronise recordings, remove unwanted intervals, and manage editing decisions.

### Audio Processing

Users should be able to select audible tracks and adjust their relative gain.

### Video Processing

Users should be able to choose visible video tracks and configure basic pan and zoom transformations.

### Preview and Export

Users should be able to preview the edited composition and export the final result locally.
